# Harbor Street incident — timeline

A fictional case file demonstrating the evidence-locker format. Every name,
date, and document here is invented.

## Tenancy

- **[evidence]** 2024-03-12 — **Lease signed** at 44 Harbor Street. Mara Voss signs a 12-month lease for the ground-floor unit; deposit $1,800. The listing was posted March 1. `listing-44`
- **[evidence]** 2024-06-02 — **Water damage reported.** Tenant reports water staining on the kitchen ceiling after heavy rain; photos filed as evidence/001-kitchen-photos.md. Landlord acknowledges by text the same day.
- 2024-06-20 — **Repair visit, no fix.** A contractor inspects, declares the flashing "probably fine," leaves without repairs. The stain spreads through July.

## Dispute

- **[evidence]** 2024-09-14 — **Mold test ordered.** Independent test ($240); result: *stachybotrys* in the ceiling cavity. Lab report filed as evidence/002-mold-report.md. `lab-report-88`
- **[allegation]** 2024-10-01 — **Rent withheld.** Tenant places October rent in escrow pending remediation, citing housing code §12-44. Landlord threatens eviction by letter dated Oct 3.
- **[evidence]** 2025-01-22 — **Mediation.** City mediation session; landlord agrees to full roof repair by March 1 and reimbursement of the mold test. Agreement filed as evidence/003-mediation-terms.md.
